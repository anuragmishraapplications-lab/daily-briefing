Treasury proposes rules to implement the GENIUS Act stablecoin framework

The Treasury Department issued a Notice of Proposed Rulemaking implementing section 3 of the GENIUS Act, the statute governing payment stablecoins. The stated aim is regulatory certainty for issuers and reinforcement of the dollar's reserve-currency role as tokenised settlement grows. The proposal moves stablecoin oversight from enforcement-by-guidance toward a codified federal regime with defined reserve and disclosure obligations. Comment periods will determine how far the rules reach non-bank issuers and offshore entities serving US customers.
