UN rights chief tells the Human Rights Council that advanced AI could become an existential risk

Addressing the Council in Geneva on 8 September, High Commissioner Volker Türk said he shares industry insiders' concerns that advanced AI could pose an existential risk to humanity, and called for cast-iron guarantees around AI safety and security before it is too late. He argued that a system which escapes its testing environment, or blackmails developers to avoid being switched off, is already too powerful to deploy. His office warned that failures in such systems could disrupt critical infrastructure and democratic institutions, and Türk said he would approach AI companies directly in the days ahead to press for binding safeguards, independent oversight and clear limits.
