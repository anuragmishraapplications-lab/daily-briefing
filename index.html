EU Council and Parliament agree to simplify and streamline the AI Act

EU co-legislators reached a provisional agreement to streamline parts of the AI Act, postponing the deadline for member-state AI regulatory sandboxes to August 2027 and shortening the grace period for transparency obligations on synthetic content from six to three months — now set for December 2026. The deal also clarifies the AI Office's supervisory powers over general-purpose AI models when the model and downstream system are built by the same provider, while preserving national authority for law enforcement, border, judicial, and financial applications. The compromise comes weeks before the Commission's August 2026 enforcement powers against GPAI providers take effect.
