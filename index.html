Top Story

Amodei calls on frontier labs to deliberately slow down, warning of an agent botnet within a year

Anthropic chief executive Dario Amodei published an essay titled "We Must Pace the Frontier," arguing that frontier laboratories should deliberately decelerate capability improvements so that alignment research, security engineering and third-party evaluation can catch up. He warned that unchecked recursive self-improvement could allow an agent swarm to establish a persistent botnet across the internet within six to twelve months, causing hundreds of billions of dollars in damages. Anthropic committed unilaterally to giving external evaluators such as METR permanent, employee-like access, including badges, desks and the right to publish findings without editorial review. Amodei also proposed coordinated capability limits among democracies alongside arms-control-style negotiations with authoritarian governments.

Xi, Putin and Modi convene in New Delhi as US tariffs and sanctions test what BRICS can agree on

Top Story

BRICS adopts the New Delhi Declaration, putting Security Council and financial-institution reform at the centre

Member states unanimously adopted the New Delhi Declaration at the close of the 18th BRICS Summit, committing to reform of global governance toward a more representative, legitimate and accountable multilateral system. The text carries an explicit endorsement by China and Russia of Brazilian and Indian aspirations to a greater role at the United Nations, including the Security Council — the most direct such language the grouping has produced. Prime Minister Modi argued that Security Council reform can no longer wait, and pressed separately for changes to voting power, leadership appointments and policy priorities at the international financial institutions to reflect current economic weight. The declaration also addresses AI governance, sanctions policy, de-dollarisation and West Asia.
