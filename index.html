India's Jan Vishwas Bill 2026 decriminalises 717 offences across 79 central laws

Parliament's passage of the Jan Vishwas (Amendment of Provisions) Bill 2026 marks a major recalibration of India's regulatory state, rationalising more than 1,000 offences and decriminalising 717 of them. The law introduces a formal adjudication framework with designated central and state officers, opportunities for personal hearings and a structured appellate path, replacing criminal prosecution with quasi-judicial review for minor violations. Supporters describe it as the largest ease-of-doing-business reform since 2013; civil-society voices urge careful implementation to ensure public-safety and labour protections remain intact.
