EU AI Act Full Enforcement Begins August 2, 2026 — High-Risk AI Systems Now Subject to Sweeping Obligations

The European Union's AI Act reaches its most significant enforcement milestone on August 2, 2026, when the full suite of obligations for high-risk AI systems comes into force. From that date, providers and deployers of high-risk AI must comply with requirements covering risk management, data governance, technical documentation, human oversight, accuracy, and cybersecurity. The European Commission simultaneously gains direct enforcement powers over general-purpose AI model providers — including the authority to demand documentation, conduct evaluations, and impose fines of up to EUR 35 million or 7% of worldwide annual turnover. A provisional "Digital Omnibus" agreement between the EU Council and Parliament, expected to be formally adopted by July, will streamline overlapping obligations and push AI regulatory sandbox deadlines to August 2027.
