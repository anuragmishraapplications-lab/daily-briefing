Federal court rules the Pentagon unlawfully retaliated against Anthropic over AI use limits

A California judge found that the Defense Department acted unlawfully when it designated Anthropic a supply-chain risk after the company declined an open-ended Pentagon contract that would have overridden its own restrictions on how Claude can be used. Anthropic had held two red lines: no knowing support for lethal autonomous weapons, and none for domestic mass surveillance. The Defense Department had responded by moving to restrict federal agencies and contractors from using the company's technology. The ruling gives AI developers meaningful legal footing when resisting government demands they consider unsafe, and raises the stakes for OpenAI, Google and Microsoft as they pursue national-security contracts while maintaining internal use policies. Separate litigation continues in Washington and the government may appeal.
