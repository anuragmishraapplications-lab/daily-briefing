Top Story

EU agrees to "AI Act Omnibus", extending deadlines and softening high-risk system rules

The Council and Parliament reached a political agreement on the AI Act Omnibus, the most consequential rewrite of Europe's flagship AI law since its passage. The deal pushes the deadline for national AI regulatory sandboxes to August 2027, narrows the scope of the Act for industrial AI already covered by the Machinery Regulation, and introduces new rules on AI-generated intimate content. The grace period for transparency labelling of synthetic content is cut from six months to three, with a hard December 2 deadline. Formal adoption is expected by July, just ahead of the August 2 trigger date for high-risk AI system obligations.
