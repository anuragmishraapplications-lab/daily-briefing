UNCAC adopts first-ever global resolution on political-finance transparency, co-sponsored by 60 countries

The 11th Conference of State Parties to the UN Convention Against Corruption adopted Resolution 11/7, the first comprehensive global standard on transparency in the funding of political parties, candidates and electoral campaigns. Tabled by Albania, Ghana, Mongolia and Norway and co-sponsored by nearly 60 countries, the resolution urges members to strengthen legal frameworks, oversight mechanisms and enforcement of political finance rules. Anti-corruption advocates called it the most consequential governance step at the UN level in years, particularly because it explicitly links political-finance opacity to democratic backsliding and procurement-level corruption.
