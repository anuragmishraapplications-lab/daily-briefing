India's Parliament Passes Tribunals Reforms Bill, Creating a National Tribunal Commission

The Rajya Sabha passed the Tribunals Reforms Bill, 2026 on August 11, after the Lok Sabha cleared it a day earlier, establishing a National Tribunal Commission to oversee the appointment, qualifications and service conditions of tribunal chairpersons and members nationwide. The government says the reform will improve efficiency and ensure greater independence, transparency and uniformity across India's tribunal system. Passage was contentious: opposition INDIA-bloc MPs walked out of the Upper House after the Leader of Opposition was not permitted to speak during the debate.
