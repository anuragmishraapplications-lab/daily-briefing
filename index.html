Top Story

Illinois enacts nation-leading AI Safety Measures Act

Governor JB Pritzker signed the Artificial Intelligence Safety Measures Act, requiring large frontier developers earning at least $500 million a year to publish safety frameworks, disclose how they assess catastrophic risk, and submit to first-in-the-nation annual independent third-party audits. Developers must report harmful incidents within 72 hours, or 24 hours where there is imminent risk to life. Modelled on California and New York bills, the three states cover roughly 40% of the US AI market, setting a de facto national standard in the absence of federal rules.
