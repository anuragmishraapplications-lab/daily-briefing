EU Digital Omnibus reshapes AI Act: centralised oversight, easier rules for small mid-caps, deadlines pushed to 2027

The European Parliament and Council have reached provisional political agreement on the Digital Omnibus, the most substantive recalibration of the AI Act since it entered into force. The package consolidates oversight of general-purpose AI systems under a strengthened AI Office, reducing fragmentation across national authorities. It extends regulatory exemptions previously granted to SMEs to small mid-cap companies, narrows requirements in a limited set of cases and permits the processing of sensitive personal data specifically for bias detection and mitigation. High-risk system rules covering biometrics, critical infrastructure, education, employment, migration and border control will now apply from 2 December 2027, with embedded systems following in August 2028.
