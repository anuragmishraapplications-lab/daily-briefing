China's economy extends its slowdown as output, retail sales and investment all miss

July data released by the National Bureau of Statistics showed weakness across the board: industrial production grew 4.5% year on year, slowing for the first time in three months, while retail sales growth fell to 0.6%. Investment fell away sharply, extending an historic slump that a booming technology sector has not been able to offset. Extreme weather compounded the problem, with heavy rain and high winds temporarily closing factories and ports and forcing evacuations. The figures follow second-quarter growth of 4.3% and revive pressure on Beijing to accelerate stimulus after months of fiscal austerity.

Top Story

The EU AI Act's penalty regime and transparency duties bite, even as high-risk rules slip to 2027

From 2 August 2026 the Act's Article 50 transparency obligations, its enforcement powers over general-purpose AI and its full penalty regime are in force across the EU. Chatbot disclosure, machine-readable marking of AI-generated content and deepfake labelling all apply now. What did move is the high-risk tier: the Digital Omnibus amendments, approved by the European Parliament in June by 423 votes to 57, defer standalone Annex III obligations covering recruitment, credit scoring, law enforcement, education and border control to December 2027, and AI embedded in regulated products to August 2028. The split creates an awkward interim regime in which penalties exist before much of the substantive compliance burden does.
