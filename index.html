EU Parliament Approves AI Act Omnibus: Deadlines Extended, Nudifier Tools Banned

The European Parliament voted 423 in favour, 57 against, and 174 abstentions to formally adopt the Digital Omnibus on AI on June 16 — a targeted amendment package that extends the full compliance deadline for high-risk AI systems from August 2026 to December 2027, giving industry a 16-month reprieve. The package also pushes back mandatory AI-generated content watermarking obligations to December 2026, and introduces a first-of-its-kind explicit prohibition on AI systems designed to produce non-consensual intimate imagery. The EU Council is expected to formally sign off and publish the legislation on June 29, triggering the countdown to new enforcement timelines. The vote reflects growing recognition across European institutions that the AI Act's original implementation schedule was overambitious for both companies and regulators.
