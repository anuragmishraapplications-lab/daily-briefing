China's foreign trade jumps 17.6 percent in the first eight months of 2026

Customs data released this week put China's combined goods imports and exports at 34.78 trillion yuan, roughly $5.13 trillion, for January through August, a 17.6 percent year-on-year rise. Exports grew 14.6 percent to 20.17 trillion yuan while imports climbed a faster 22 percent to 14.61 trillion yuan, narrowing the historic gap between the two flows. August alone saw exports up 25 percent year on year, accelerating from 23.9 percent in July, with AI-related hardware shipments cited as a major driver. The offshore yuan held near 6.70 per dollar, close to its strongest level since January 2023.

UK Health Bill reaches report stage with the abolition of NHS England on the table

The Health Bill 2026-27 entered its report stage in the Commons on Monday, opening debate on the government's principal structural reforms to English health administration. Day one covered abolishing NHS England and transferring its functions back to the department, restructuring Integrated Care Boards and NHS foundation trusts, and establishing a Single Patient Record. The Single Patient Record is the most consequential digital-governance element, consolidating patient data into one national record with attendant questions about access controls and consent. The bill represents the largest reorganisation of NHS governance in over a decade.
