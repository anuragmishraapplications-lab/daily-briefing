EU's Digital Omnibus reform of the AI Act clears its final hurdle

Regulation (EU) 2026/1744 — the Digital Omnibus reform of the AI Act — was published in the Official Journal on 24 July and entered into force on 27 July, reshaping Europe's flagship AI rulebook. The package extends compliance deadlines for high-risk AI systems, introduces a new "agentic AI" category, and adds rules on AI-generated intimate content while cutting red tape for SMEs. Obligations for high-risk uses in biometrics, critical infrastructure, education and employment are now pushed to December 2027, a major delay from the original August 2026 timeline. Supporters call it pragmatic simplification; critics warn it dilutes protections.
