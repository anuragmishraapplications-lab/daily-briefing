Top Story

The EU AI Act crosses from rulemaking into enforcement

With the Act fully applicable since 2 August, August 2026 marks the point at which European AI regulation stopped being aspirational. The EU AI Office has issued its first penalty decisions, and every member state was required to stand up at least one national regulatory sandbox by the same deadline. Penalties reach €35 million or 7 percent of global turnover, whichever is higher. Alongside FDA enforcement letters in the United States and movement on federal AI liability, the shift sets a compliance baseline that any lab or deployer operating in Europe now has to meet in practice rather than on paper.
