EU designates ChatGPT a Very Large Online Search Engine under the Digital Services Act

The European Commission classified ChatGPT as a VLOSE, making OpenAI’s chatbot the first standalone AI service pulled under the bloc’s strictest platform regime. The designation rests on ChatGPT’s live web-search function rather than its status as an AI product, and follows a reported 159.1 million average monthly EU users against a 45 million threshold. OpenAI has four months — to January 2027 — to meet the added obligations: systemic risk assessments, independent audits, an advertising repository and researcher data access. It is the clearest signal yet that Europe intends to regulate assistants as information gatekeepers, not merely as models.
