Top Story

EU begins enforcing AI Act transparency rules: chatbots and deepfakes must now identify themselves

From 2 August 2026, the European Commission's AI Office and national authorities began enforcing the EU AI Act's transparency obligations under Article 50 — the first continent-wide rules requiring AI systems to disclose themselves to the humans they interact with. Chatbots must tell users they are not human, and AI-generated or altered content, including deepfakes, must carry machine-readable marks. Non-compliance can trigger fines of up to €15 million or 3% of global turnover. Providers of systems already on the market have until December to conform.
