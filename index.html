European Commission begins enforcing AI Act transparency obligations

The Commission's AI Office, working with national authorities, started enforcing the AI Act's transparency requirements on 2 August, moving the regulation from statute into supervised practice. Providers of general-purpose models now face disclosure duties on training data, systemic-risk assessment and incident reporting. Bans on manipulative systems, social scoring and real-time public facial recognition are already in force. Enforcement capacity across member states remains uneven, which will determine how much the obligations actually bind.
