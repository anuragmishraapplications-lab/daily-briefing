Independent benchmarks put GPT-6 "Astra" far below OpenAI's headline score

OpenAI's GPT-6 Astra reached paying subscribers on 4 September, but independent evaluation the following day complicated the launch narrative. Top Story

UK MPs take up amendments to the Representation of the People Bill

Parliament spent the week of 1–4 September on amendments to the Government's Representation of the People Bill, the most substantial rewrite of UK election law, political finance and campaigning rules in years. The same sitting saw MPs debate a motion authorising legislation to reform the Sovereign Grant, the public funding stream behind the Monarch's official duties — a commitment made in this year's King's Speech. Taken together the two items make this an unusually consequential week for constitutional plumbing: how campaigns are financed and how the head of state is funded are both being reopened in the same session, and both will shape the rules for the next general election.
