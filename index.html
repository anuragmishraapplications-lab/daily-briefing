Top Story

California enacts the first US laws requiring independent AI audits

Governor Newsom signed AB 1405, creating the nation's first state-run registry for AI auditors, alongside SB 813, which establishes a framework for independent verification organisations that can assess AI systems for compliance with state law. The registry must be online by 1 January 2029, after which no one without a state registration number may conduct a covered audit. The independence rules have teeth: auditors cannot evaluate systems they materially designed or operated, and staff generally cannot audit an area where they held material responsibility at the client in the previous twelve months. Violations mean removal from the registry and referral to the attorney general. It is the clearest attempt yet to build the professional infrastructure that AI regulation has so far assumed but never created.
